1. The spirit of the garden

Lumina’s reviews, replies, shared collections and watch parties exist to help people find and enjoy films and series. Write the kind of review you would want to read: honest, specific and kind to the people you are talking to, even when you are critical of the work.

2. What is welcome

  • Honest opinions about a title, positive or negative, with reasons.
  • Context that helps others decide: pacing, tone, content that some viewers may want to know about, accessibility of the audio or subtitles.
  • Disagreement with other reviewers that stays about the work, not the person.
  • Short quotations from a title when they support your point.

3. What is not allowed

  • Spam and advertising: promotional links, repeated or copy-pasted posts, offers, contact details for off-platform deals, or posts unrelated to the title.
  • Harassment and bullying: insults, threats, pile-ons, or targeting another member or a creator personally.
  • Hate: attacking or dehumanising people because of a protected characteristic such as race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.
  • Sexual content that is explicit, and any sexual content involving minors.
  • Violence: threats, glorifying real-world violence, or encouraging self-harm.
  • Misinformation likely to cause real-world harm.
  • Private information: sharing someone’s personal details (addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, documents) without permission.
  • Impersonation of another member, a creator, Lumina staff or anyone else.
  • Copyright infringement: posting material you do not have the right to share. See the Copyright & Takedown policy.
  • Unmarked spoilers: if your review reveals important plot points, tick “Contains spoilers” so it is hidden until a reader chooses to reveal it.
  • Manipulating ratings: reviewing titles you have not engaged with in order to raise or lower their member rating, or coordinating others to do so.

4. Reporting

If you are signed in, you can report a review, reply or shared collection and choose a reason: spam, harassment, hate, spoilers, sexual content, violence, misinformation, copyright or other. You can add details to help moderators. Reports are confidential: the author is not told who reported them.

When three different members report the same review or reply, it is hidden automatically until a moderator looks at it. Reported shared collections stay as they are until a moderator decides. You can also block a review author so their reviews no longer appear in your review lists.

5. How moderation works

  • Spam filter. An automatic filter scores new reviews and replies for signs of spam (for example links, advertising phrases, repeated text or many posts in a short time). A post that scores too high is held for a moderator instead of appearing immediately. The filter never deletes anything.
  • Human review. Moderators see reports grouped by the item reported, with a preview of the content. They can dismiss a report, hide the content, remove it, or suspend the author’s account.
  • Hide or remove. Hidden and removed reviews disappear from title pages and stop counting toward the title’s member rating straight away.
  • Suspension. For serious or repeated breaches an account can be suspended, either until a date or indefinitely. A suspended account is signed out everywhere.
  • Notice. When your content is hidden or removed, or your account is suspended, you receive a notification explaining the decision.
  • Accountability. Every moderation decision is recorded in an internal audit log with the moderator who made it.

6. Appeals

If you think a decision about your content or account was wrong, write to [Moderation appeals email] with a link to the content or the date of the notification. Where possible, a different member of staff will review the decision. We aim to reply within [Appeal response period].

7. Kids profiles

Reviews and replies are turned off on kids profiles. Parents and guardians manage kids profiles, their age limits and PINs from the account.

8. Watch parties

Watch parties are invite-only. The same rules apply to party chat. Hosts can end a party at any time. Chat is not saved after the party ends, so if something serious happens, report it to [Moderation appeals email] with the time and the party details.

9. Changes

We may update these guidelines as the community grows. Significant changes will be announced in the service before they take effect.
