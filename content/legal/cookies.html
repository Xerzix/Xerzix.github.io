1. Summary

  • Lumina sets one cookie, lumina_sid, and only when you sign in. It is strictly necessary to keep you signed in.
  • Lumina uses no analytics, advertising, social-media or tracking cookies, and no similar technologies such as tracking pixels or fingerprinting.
  • Your preferences and, in Preview mode, your list and progress are kept in your browser’s local storage and session storage. These entries never leave your device unless the feature that uses them sends them as part of a request you make (described below).
  • Because everything Lumina stores is needed for features you use, there is no consent banner. If analytics or advertising cookies were ever added, we would ask for your consent first and update this policy.

2. The session cookie

Cookies set by Lumina
NamePurposeTypeLifetime
lumina_sid Keeps you signed in and remembers the profile you selected. It holds a random token; the server stores only a hash of it. It is HttpOnly (page scripts cannot read it), SameSite=Lax, and Secure when the service runs over HTTPS. First-party, strictly necessary Up to 30 days. Deleted when you sign out, when the session is revoked from Account settings or by staff, or when it expires.

In Preview mode (static hosting) Lumina has no accounts and sets no cookies at all.

You can remove Lumina’s entries from this device at any time in Settings → Privacy & data → “Clear data stored on this device”, or through your browser’s site-data settings. Clearing them resets your on-device preferences; on the Lumina server, your account data is not affected.

4. Third-party hosts

Fonts, icons and the video player library are served from this site, so no font or content-delivery service is contacted. When you play one of the openly licensed films that are streamed from a third-party host (listed in the Privacy Policy), your browser fetches the video from that host. Lumina does not ask those hosts to set cookies, but their own policies apply to the requests they receive.

5. Your choices

  • Blocking the lumina_sid cookie will prevent you from signing in, but you can still browse and play the open catalog.
  • Blocking local storage keeps Lumina working, but your appearance choices and on-device data will not be remembered between visits.
  • Private or incognito windows discard all of the above when you close them.

6. Changes

If Lumina starts using a new cookie or storage entry, we will list it here before it is used, and we will ask for consent first if it is not strictly necessary.
