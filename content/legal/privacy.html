1. Summary

  • We collect what is needed to run your account and profiles, remember what you watch, show community features and keep the service secure.
  • We do not sell personal data. Lumina has no advertising, no analytics service and no tracking pixels, and it sets only two cookies: one that keeps you signed in, and one that lets “Who’s watching?” list the identities used on your browser.
  • Fonts, icons and the video player library are served from this site. Your browser contacts other hosts only when you play one of the openly licensed films that are streamed from third-party hosts (see section 6).
  • You can download your data and delete your account yourself in Account settings.
  • In Preview mode (static hosting) there are no accounts: everything you save stays in your browser on that device.

3. Velvia Suggestions

Velvia Suggestions recommends titles from this catalog in conversation. How your messages are handled depends on how the operator has configured it:

  • Built-in engine (default). Your messages are processed on the Lumina server only. They are not stored on the server.
  • External AI provider (only if configured). If the operator connects an external AI provider ([AI provider name, if configured]), the text of your current conversation and catalog information about the titles being discussed (titles, synopses, genres, credits, ratings and similar metadata) are sent to that provider to generate a reply. If the profile’s “Use viewing history for recommendations” setting is on, the names of up to ten titles that profile recently watched are also sent, along with which candidate titles it has already watched. This setting is on by default; you can turn it off in Settings → Privacy & data, or for a single conversation in Velvia. Your email address, account details and profile name are never sent. [Describe the provider’s data processing terms and location.]

The conversation is kept in your browser’s session storage for the current tab so you can continue it, and is cleared when the tab is closed.

4. What other people can see

  • Reviews and replies are public on title pages, under your profile name and avatar. Your email address is never shown.
  • Collections are private unless you make them “unlisted”, in which case anyone with the link can see the collection and your profile name.
  • In a watch party, other members see your profile name, avatar and chat messages. Party chat is kept only in the server’s memory while the party exists (up to the last 50 messages) and is not saved to the database.
  • Lumina staff (moderators and administrators) can see account and moderation information needed for their role. Their access requires a recent password confirmation and every privileged action is recorded in the audit log.

5. Preview mode (static hosting)

When Lumina runs in Preview mode — for example a copy published on a static web host — there is no Lumina server and no accounts. Your list, progress, history, collections and preferences are stored only in your browser’s local storage on that device, and you can clear them in Settings → Privacy & data. The static host that serves the files receives ordinary web request information (such as your IP address and browser) under its own privacy policy: [Static hosting provider and policy link].

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal data, to receive it in a portable format and to withdraw consent. Lumina implements the most common requests directly:

  • Access and portability: Account settings → “Download your data” gives you a JSON file with your account, profiles, library, collections, reviews, replies, reports, notifications, quality reports, creator applications and submissions.
  • Deletion: Account settings → “Delete account…” deletes your account and personal data immediately, except the records described in section 7. You can also delete individual history entries, reviews, replies, collections and notifications at any time.
  • Correction: change your display name, email address, profiles and preferences in Account settings and Settings.
  • Choices: turn off history-based recommendations and viewing statistics per profile, switch off optional notification types, stop saving watch progress, and clear search history and data stored on this device.
  • Other requests (for example objection or restriction) can be sent to [Privacy contact email]. We will respond within [Response period].

You may also complain to a data protection authority: [Supervisory authority and contact details].

9. Security

  • Passwords and profile PINs are stored only as salted scrypt hashes; two-factor secrets are encrypted at rest.
  • The session and device cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite, are marked Secure when the service runs over HTTPS, and only hashes of their tokens are stored.
  • If the operator enables artwork from The Movie Database (TMDB), the Lumina server fetches posters from TMDB and serves them from its own address; your browser does not contact TMDB and no personal data is sent to it. (On static Preview hosting, posters load directly from TMDB’s image servers, which then see your IP address.)
  • Requests that change data are protected against cross-site request forgery; sign-in, reporting and similar actions are rate limited.
  • Uploaded files are stored outside the public web folder, checked by their content (not just their name) and served to staff only through short-lived signed links.
  • Staff tools require a recent password confirmation (and two-factor authentication when the operator requires it), and every privileged action is recorded.
  • The site uses a strict Content Security Policy and builds its pages without inserting untrusted HTML.

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please tell us at [Security contact email]. If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

10. Children

Lumina accounts are for adults. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under [Minimum age]. A parent or guardian can create kids profiles inside their own account, set an age limit and protect profiles with a PIN. Data about a kids profile (such as its list and viewing history) belongs to the parent’s account and is managed, exported and deleted by the account holder. Reviews and replies are turned off on kids profiles. If you believe a child has created an account, contact [Privacy contact email] and we will delete it.
