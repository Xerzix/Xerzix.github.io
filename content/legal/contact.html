1. Help yourself first

Many things can be done straight away in Lumina, without waiting for a reply:

  • Forgot your password? Use “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes.
  • Suspicious activity? In Account settings, review your signed-in sessions, sign out the ones you do not recognise, change your password and turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Your data: download a copy of it or delete your account in Account settings.
  • Notifications: choose which optional notifications you receive in Settings → Notifications.
  • Kids and parental controls: manage profiles, age limits and PINs from “Manage profiles”.
  • A title does not play well? Use “Report a playback problem” in the player. You can choose to attach the player’s measurements, which helps us find the cause.
  • A review or reply breaks the rules? Use “Report” on it. See the Community Guidelines.

4. Staying safe

  • Lumina staff will never ask for your password, your two-factor codes or your profile PINs — not by email, in a review reply or in a watch party.
  • Messages from Lumina about your account appear in your notification centre. If an email claims to be from us, you can check for a matching notice there.
  • Lumina does not ask for payment details.
