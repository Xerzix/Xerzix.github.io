7. Reviews and moderation

  • Member ratings are calculated from reviews that are visible. Hidden or removed reviews do not count.
  • An automatic filter may hold a review or reply that looks like spam (for example, repeated text, many posts in a short time, links or advertising phrases). Held posts are not deleted; they wait for a person to review them.
  • Any signed-in member can report a review, reply or shared collection. When three different members report the same review or reply, it is hidden automatically until a moderator reviews it. Reported collections are reviewed by a moderator.
  • Moderators can dismiss a report, hide or remove content, or suspend the author. Every moderation decision is recorded in an internal audit log. When content is hidden or removed, or an account is suspended, the author receives a notification explaining the decision.
  • You can block a review author so their reviews no longer appear in your review lists.
  • If you believe a decision was wrong, contact us at [Moderation appeals email] with the link to the content. A different member of staff will look at it where possible.
